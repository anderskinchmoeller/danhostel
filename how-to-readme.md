# Actual lodging revenue · quick notes

**Where:** Resultater → Importér afsluttet dagsresultat.

## Upload

- Same completed stay date, after the night run.
- **Hotelmanager:** parts 1 + 2 (Room/Bed); exclude VAT, packages, credit and out-of-order.
- **Product Turnover:** Overnight; split by room type; exclude VAT.
  - Include Confirmed, Guaranteed, In-House and Out of House.
  - “Arrival date” → verify that amounts cover the intended stay night.
- Check counts, breakfast deductions and totals → confirm.
- Counts can be confirmed separately; uploading only creates a draft.

## If totals differ

- Add **Accounts summary** for the same date.
- Investigate using **All transactions**:
  - **1002:** lodging, discounts and refunds.
  - **1004:** upgrades.
  - **1005:** breakfast.
- Account numbers → account; Exclude off; no product restriction.
- Transaction PDFs are supporting evidence; upload field expects the summary.
- Keep “None” amounts separate; verify stay dates and references.
- MM packages can include breakfast; do not guess room-type allocations.
- Do not add Accounts totals on top of Product Turnover.
